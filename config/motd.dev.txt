Welcome to DevNet, an IRC network for developers and IT staff.
The server is irc.dev. It speaks RFC 1459/2812 and the IRCv3
extensions this ircd implements. Read /ADMIN for the operators.

Where to talk:
  #lobby           introductions and "where do I ask?"
  #announcements   staff notices (moderated)
  #helpdesk        accounts, devices, mail, VPN, access
  #dev             design, debugging, implementation
  #review          a specific change
  #ops             deploys and reliability
  #incident        a live incident
  #infra           hosts, containers, storage, platform
  #net             DNS, routing, firewalls, TLS
  #security        defensive security
  #data            schemas, queries, migrations, backups
  #docs            runbooks, decisions, status notes
  #random          off topic

Assistants in those rooms are bots. Their answers are generated
by a model and are not an operator decision. Address one with
"nick: your question", or reply to its message. Do not paste
passwords, tokens, or private keys. Incident lines may be sent
to the model when oncall answers; review lines, when reviewer does.

Be specific, stay in the channel's topic, and be kind.
